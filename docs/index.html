EXPERIENCE & CAPABILITIES

Every Feature Built Into the Capsule

Everything runs off a single deterministic coroutine state coordinator (IslandStateCoordinator) persisted via Jetpack DataStore Preferences—keeping the dashboard stage and system overlay perfectly in sync.

Live Notifications & Inline Reply

Compact heads-up pill displays sender avatar/app icon and title. Tap or swipe down to expand the full message body, trigger notification actions, or send an inline RemoteInput reply without leaving your current app.

RemoteInput Action Buttons Deep Link Launch

Music & Media Visualizer

Automatically syncs with active Android MediaSession playback—showing album artwork, track & artist metadata, animated 5-bar frequency waveform, live elapsed progress, interactive seek scrubber, and Previous / Play-Pause / Next controls.

MediaSession Live Waveform Seek Scrubber

Calls, Timers & Split-Pill Mode

Tracks incoming & ongoing calls and countdown timers with tabular (tnum) chronometers. When two activities run simultaneously (e.g., music + timer or call + media), the capsule automatically splits into a primary pill + secondary detached mini-pill.

Dual Split-Pill Live Chronometer Incoming Popups

Charging, Bluetooth & Ringer Popups

Instant transient popups for charger plug-in (AC, USB, Wireless, fast-charging & battery %), Bluetooth headphones/watches/speakers with battery %, and ringer mode switches (SILENT, VIBRATE, NORMAL).

2s–8s Auto-Dismiss Fast Charge Badge BT Battery %

Smart Visibility & Pin Preview Lab

Hide When Idle removes the capsule completely when nothing is active. Hide in Landscape auto-collapses during fullscreen video & games. Show on Lockscreen keeps live events accessible, and Pin Preview locks mock events on screen while you calibrate dimensions.

Hide When Idle Hide in Landscape Pin Preview

Privacy-First Accessibility Overlay

Uses TYPE_ACCESSIBILITY_OVERLAY exclusively to render the floating capsule above the status bar and camera cutout across apps. Does not inspect, log, or transmit screen text or passwords—100% offline & open source.

Zero Analytics No Internet Required Spring Physics

ENGINEERING

Clean Reactive Android Architecture

Built with Kotlin coroutines, StateFlow, Jetpack Compose inside an Accessibility Service window host, and unit-tested priority transitions.

SYSTEM LISTENERS

IslandNotificationListenerService & SystemEventsMonitor

Captures notifications, RemoteInput, MediaSession, calls, timers, charging, Bluetooth & ringer state.

STATE MACHINE

IslandStateCoordinator

Deterministic StateFlow coordinator resolving priority, dual split-pill pairing, timeouts, and lock/landscape rules.

PERSISTENCE & THEME

SettingsRepository & Theme.kt

Jetpack DataStore Preferences storing per-feature dimensions, offsets, visibility toggles, and the 3 synchronized IslandPalette worlds.

COMPOSE OVERLAY

IslandAccessibilityService & DynamicIslandOverlay

Hosts Compose UI on TYPE_ACCESSIBILITY_OVERLAY with spring animations, gesture detectors, and live waveform rendering.
